Common questions

How do I cancel my subscription?
On your iPhone: Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Paydar › Cancel Subscription. Cancelling stops renewal; you keep access until the end of the paid period.

I reinstalled the app. How do I get my purchase back?
Open the paywall and tap Restore Purchases, using the same Apple Account you bought with.

How do I get my data back on a new iPhone?
Turn on Cloud Backup (Settings › Cloud Backup & Account) and sign in with Apple or Google on both devices. Without Cloud Backup your data lives only on the original iPhone; export a backup from Settings › Export first.

How do I delete my account?
Settings › Cloud Backup & Account › Delete account & cloud data. This deletes your cloud data and account immediately. Data on your iPhone stays until you delete the app.

Is Paydar's number official?
No. Paydar gives estimates to help you spot mistakes. It is not legal, tax or financial advice; wage rules vary by state and employer.

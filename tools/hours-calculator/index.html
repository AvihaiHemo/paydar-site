How to count hours between two times

Subtract the start time from the end time, then take off unpaid breaks. From 8:30 AM to 5:15 PM is 8 hours 45 minutes; with a 30-minute lunch it is 8 hours 15 minutes. A shift that crosses midnight works too: 10:00 PM to 6:00 AM is 8 hours.

Hours and minutes to decimal hours

Payroll uses decimals. Divide the minutes by 60: 15 minutes is 0.25, 30 is 0.5, 45 is 0.75. So 8 hours 15 minutes is 8.25 hours.

Questions

How many hours is 9 to 5?

8 hours. With a 30-minute unpaid lunch it is 7 hours 30 minutes, or 7.5 hours.

How do I write 7 hours 45 minutes as a decimal?

7.75 hours. 45 minutes divided by 60 is 0.75.

Does the calculator handle overnight shifts?

Yes. If the end time is earlier than the start time, it counts the shift as ending the next day.

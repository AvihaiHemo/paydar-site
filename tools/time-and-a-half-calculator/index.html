What time and a half means

Time and a half is 1.5 times your regular hourly rate. At $20 an hour it is $30 an hour. Under federal law most hourly workers earn it for every hour over 40 in a workweek. Some states also require it after 8 hours in one day.

How to calculate it

Multiply your hourly rate by 1.5, then by the overtime hours. Six overtime hours at $20 an hour is 6 × $30 = $180. Of that, $60 is the extra half on top of regular pay.

Double time

Double time is 2 times your rate. Federal law does not require it. California requires it after 12 hours in a day and after 8 hours on the seventh day in a row of a workweek. Elsewhere it is company policy or a union contract.

Questions

What is time and a half for $15 an hour?

$22.50 an hour. Multiply the hourly rate by 1.5.

What is time and a half for $20 an hour?

$30 an hour.

Do I get time and a half on holidays or weekends?

Not under federal law. Extra pay for holidays or weekends is company policy, unless those hours take you over 40 in the week.

Is time and a half taxed more?

No. Overtime pay is taxed like the rest of your wages; a bigger check can mean more withheld. For tax years 2025 to 2028 there is a US deduction for the premium part of overtime pay, up to a yearly limit.

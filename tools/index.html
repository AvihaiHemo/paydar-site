Quick answers for hourly workers. No sign-up, nothing stored: the numbers stay in your browser.

Overtime calculatorEnter the hours you worked each day. You get total hours in hours and minutes, in decimals, and your gross pay.Is my paycheck right?Enter the hours you worked in each week of the pay period and the gross pay printed on your paystub. You will see what you should have earned and the gap, if there is one.Time clock rounding calculatorMany employers round punches to the nearest quarter hour. Enter your real times to see the rounded shift and what the difference is worth over a year.Tip out calculatorEnter tonight's tips and sales and your restaurant's tip-out percentages. You will see what goes to each role, what you keep and what the shift paid per hour.Nanny pay calculatorEnter your rate, the guaranteed hours in your agreement and the hours you worked this week.

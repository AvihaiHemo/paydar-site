Gross pay, before taxes

These are gross amounts: pay before taxes and deductions. Your take-home pay depends on your tax withholding, benefits and state, so it will be lower.

How the numbers are worked out

Weekly pay is your rate times your hours, with hours over 40 paid at 1.5 times the rate. Biweekly pay is two weeks. Yearly pay is the weekly amount times the weeks you work, and monthly pay is the yearly amount divided by 12.

At $20 an hour and 40 hours a week: $800 a week, $1,600 every two weeks, about $3,467 a month and $41,600 a year.

Why is my paycheck different from this?

This shows gross pay. Taxes and deductions come out of it. If the gross pay on your stub is lower than your hours come to, compare your hours with the paycheck checker.

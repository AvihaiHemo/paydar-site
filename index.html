How it works

Track as you work. When payday comes, you already know the number.

1

Clock in with one tap

From the app, the Lock Screen, a widget or Siri. Or let Paydar clock you in when you arrive at work.

2

See your pay build up

Overtime, tips, breaks, night differentials and guaranteed hours are worked out for you, for every job.

3

Check your paystub

Type in the gross pay on your stub. Paydar compares it with your hours and shows the gap and the likely cause.

Keep your own record

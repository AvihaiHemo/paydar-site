How it works

1. Clock in with one tapFrom the app, the Lock Screen, a widget or Siri. Or let Paydar clock you in when you arrive at work.
2. See your pay build upOvertime, tips, breaks, night differentials and guaranteed hours are worked out for you, for every job.
3. Check your paystubType in the gross pay on your stub. Paydar compares it with your hours and shows the gap and the likely cause.
4. Keep your own recordExport timesheets as PDF, Excel or CSV. Your data stays on your iPhone, with optional Cloud Backup.
